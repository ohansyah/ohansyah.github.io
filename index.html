Project 07

KPJ Official Merchandise Store

Merchandise for Komunitas Perantau Jepara sold well (about 2,500 pieces in one sale), but the money moved without a clear record and no one wanted to hold it. I built a stock-based store where every rupiah is traceable and the admin never touches the money, aimed at members who are mostly non-technical and phone-first.

  • Payments That Can't Be Faked
    An order becomes paid only from verified Midtrans data: signature check, status re-fetch, exact amount match, idempotent on gateway ref. There is no "mark as paid" button anywhere.
  • No Overselling, No Lost Records
    Stock is row-locked at order time and restored on expiry or cancellation. A single state machine controls every status change, and orders and payments are never hard-deleted.
  • Built for Non-Technical Members
    Google login, cascading province-to-village address dropdowns, an order timeline, and WhatsApp notifications. Payment, shipping and WhatsApp providers sit behind swappable drivers.
